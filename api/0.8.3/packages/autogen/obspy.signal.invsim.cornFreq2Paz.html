obspy.signal.invsim.cornFreq2Paz¶

cornFreq2Paz(fc, damp=0.707)[source]¶

Convert corner frequency and damping to poles and zeros. 2 zeros at position (0j, 0j) are given as output (m/s).

Parameters:
  • fc Corner frequency
  • damping Corner frequency
Returns:

Dictionary containing poles, zeros and gain
